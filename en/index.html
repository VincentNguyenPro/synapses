          Three steps, no account

          1. First, request your export.

            On LinkedIn: Settings, Data privacy, “Get a copy of your data”. Take the full archive to see everything, or just “Connections”, which is faster. Open the LinkedIn page ↗

          2. Then drop the archive.

            The .zip as is, once LinkedIn’s email arrives: about ten minutes for “Connections”, up to 24 hours for the full archive. It’s read on your device.

          3. Finally, explore your map.

            Group, color, filter, open a contact’s profile. While you wait for the email, the demo already works.

            Questions

            Is it really free?

            Yes. No account, no subscription, no ads. Only the optional AI classification uses your own key, with the provider you choose.

            Is Synapses linked to LinkedIn?

            No. It’s an independent tool, not affiliated with LinkedIn. It doesn’t connect to your account: it only reads the export you download.

            How long does the export take?

            Requesting it takes two minutes. The connections archive arrives by email, usually about ten minutes later; the full archive, up to 24 hours.

            What if an industry is guessed wrong?

            Industry, function and seniority are estimates. Correct them in the contact’s profile: the correction applies to the whole company or job title. You can also refine them with an AI, using your own key.

            Can I try it without my export?

            Yes. The demo opens straight onto a fictional network of 150 contacts, with every feature.

            What about on a phone?

            The graph adapts to the screen, and a List view shows everything as text. To get and drop the archive, a computer is still easier.
