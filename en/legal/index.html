Legal notice and privacy

Synapses is free, with no account and no ads. Your network stays in your browser: this page lists everything that leaves it, and why.

Updated: 5 November 2026

Publisher and hosting

  • Publisher: Vincent Nguyen, private individual, non-commercial.
  • Contact: the “Give feedback” button on the home page (or “Avis” in the tool). Tick “I’d like a reply by email” and enter your email.
  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages, GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States.

Your LinkedIn network

  • Your export is read in your browser. It is sent to no server: Synapses has no access to it.
  • Your contacts’ names, profiles and emails are never sent. Emails are never stored.
  • From your messages, only the number of exchanges and the date of the last one are read, never their text.
  • If you choose “Garder sur cet appareil” (keep on this device), your network (without emails) is stored in this browser. “Oublier mon réseau” (forget my network) or “Effacer mes données” (erase my data) deletes it.

This data is stored with Supabase, on servers in the European Union. Like any web server, Supabase and GitHub see your IP address and may keep it for a few days in their technical logs, for security.

Third-party services

  • Company logos (Brandfetch): option ticked by default, can be unticked in Display. When ticked, each logo shown sends Brandfetch the company’s website (for example doctolib.fr) and your IP address, never a contact’s name.
  • AI classification: only if you start it, with your own key. Unrecognised company names and job titles go directly to the provider you chose (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), under its terms. Your key is kept in this browser only if you tick “Se souvenir de ma clé” (remember my key).
  • Tool libraries (D3, PapaParse, JSZip): loaded from cdnjs (Cloudflare), which sees your IP address, as with any web page.
  • These services may be located outside the European Union, notably in the United States.

Storage on your device

  • No cookies, no ad trackers, no analytics.
  • Your browser keeps your settings (views, corrections, tags, recent searches) and, if you choose, your network and your AI key. This data stays on your device.
  • “Effacer mes données” (erase my data), in the tool (“Moi” card, “Tes données”), deletes it.

Your rights

  • You can ask to access, correct or erase your data, or object to its processing: write through “Give feedback” and leave your email. Reply within one month.
  • With no accounts, Synapses can’t tell which feedback is yours: give the date and the start of your message.
  • You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority, or your local authority: cnil.fr.
